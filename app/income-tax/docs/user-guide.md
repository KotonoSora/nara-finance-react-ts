# Personal Income Tax Calculator 2025 - User Guide

## Overview

This tax calculator helps estimate your 2025 income tax for multiple countries including the United States, United Kingdom, Canada, Japan, South Korea, Vietnam, and Singapore.

## Key Features

- Multi-country tax calculation support
- Bilingual interface (English, Vietnamese, Japanese)
- Support for both residents and non-residents
- Detailed tax breakdowns with interactive visualizations
- Comprehensive deductions and credits system

## Understanding Residency Status

### Residents

- Access to full tax deductions and credits
- Progressive tax rates based on income brackets
- Family deductions and insurance contributions available
- State tax calculations (US only)

### Non-Residents

- Flat tax rates apply:
  - US: 30%
  - UK: 45%
  - Canada: 25%
  - Japan: 20.42%
  - Korea: 19%
  - Vietnam: 20%
  - Singapore: 22%
- Limited access to deductions and credits
- Simplified tax calculation process

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What qualifies as a dependent?

Requirements vary by country:

- US: Must be under 19 (24 if student) or permanently disabled
- Vietnam: Dependents include children under 18, elderly parents, disabled relatives
- Japan: Must be under 16 or disabled family members
- Other countries: Check local tax regulations

### How are state taxes calculated? (US only)

State taxes are calculated separately using state-specific tax brackets:

- California: 9 tax brackets (1% - 12.3%)
- New York: 7 tax brackets (4% - 9.65%)
- Only available for residents

### What's included in Additional Income?

- Investment income (dividends, capital gains)
- Rental income
- Business income
- Other taxable income sources

### How is the Effective Tax Rate calculated?

Effective Tax Rate = (Total Tax / Gross Income) × 100
This represents your actual tax burden as a percentage of total income.

## Tax Calculation Process

1. Determine residency status and applicable country
2. Enter annual income and dependent information
3. Select itemized deductions if applicable
4. Add any tax credits
5. Review detailed tax breakdown:
   - Gross income
   - Total deductions
   - Taxable income
   - Tax by brackets
   - Final tax amount
   - Effective tax rate

## Tips for Accurate Calculations

1. Keep records of all income sources
2. Document qualifying deductions and expenses
3. Understand residency requirements
4. Consider state tax implications (US)
5. Review country-specific rules and limitations

## Important Notes

- All calculations are estimates based on projected 2025 tax rates
- Tax laws and rates may change
- Consult a qualified tax professional for official tax advice
- Non-resident calculations use simplified flat rates
- Some deductions may have caps or limitations
- State tax calculations are available only for US residents
